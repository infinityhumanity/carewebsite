About us

Caring is who we are

Infinity Home Care was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves to grow older with dignity, comfort and company — right where they feel most at home.

Our mission

A passion for serving our community

Infinity Home Care is a provider of compassionate caretakers. Caring for our neighbors and doing meaningful social work for the community has always been our passion — it's the reason we exist.

We pair every family with caregivers who are not only skilled, but genuinely kind — people who show up, listen, and treat your loved ones the way they'd treat their own.

What we believe

Care should be reachable for every family

We know quality care can feel out of reach. A big part of what we do is helping families understand the financial-assistance options available to them.

Compassion first

We lead with empathy. Every plan starts by listening to what your family actually needs.

Affordable access

We help you navigate Medicaid and other programs that can make care affordable.

Trust & safety

Screened, background-checked caregivers and regular quality check-ins give you peace of mind.

Happy and healthy at home

That's our promise, and it's at the heart of everything we do.
