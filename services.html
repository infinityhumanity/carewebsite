Our services

Care tailored to your needs

Every person is different. We offer a full range of non-medical in-home services and combine them into a plan that's just right for you or your loved one.

Homemaking

Keeping a home running smoothly so it stays safe, clean and comfortable.

  • Light house cleaning
  • Cooking & meal prep
  • Laundry
  • Grocery shopping
  • Running errands, with or without the client

Non-Medical Physical Care

Respectful, hands-on help with the daily activities that keep people independent and dignified.

  • Bathing & grooming
  • Dressing
  • Toileting
  • Walking assistance (walker / cane)
  • Transfers to and from wheelchair / bed

Companion Care

Friendship and a watchful eye — because no one should feel alone.

  • Supervision for safety & companionship
  • Assistance with activities of daily living
  • Conversation & social engagement
  • Medication reminders

Personal Care

A dedicated plan that blends physical care and homemaking around each person's unique routine, preferences and health goals.

Memory Care

Calm, patient, specialized support for clients living with Alzheimer's or dementia — with routines that bring comfort and reduce confusion.
